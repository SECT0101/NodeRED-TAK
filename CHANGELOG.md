# Changelog

NodeRED-TAK versions. Newest first.

## 1.2.0
- First public release: 19 ready-to-import Node-RED flows that put free public data on the TAK map as CoT.
- **Weather and hazards**: NHC tropical weather, USGS earthquakes and water gauges, NWS river forecasts and local storm reports, FEMA disaster declarations and open shelters, wildfire incidents and perimeters, GOES lightning (GLM), emergency and wildfire squawks.
- **Space**: weather satellites, space stations, amateur radio satellites, space weather.
- **Radio**: Winlink RMS gateways, Meshtastic nodes, APRS stations, Marinesia AIS vessels.
- **Mapping**: CalTopo.
